1. Rank 1 → 5 Progression

All 5 abilities start at Rank 1. Climbing to Rank 5 takes real sustained effort. Fitness Rank 5 is scientifically gated by a healthy BMI for your height.

2. Multi-Stat + Skill XP

Every task scales XP by difficulty (Trivial → Heroic) and can boost multiple abilities + skills at once (e.g., Reading Sartre → Culture + Productivity + Philosophy).

3. 3 Recurring Modes

Days of Week (Mon/Tue, weekends), Spaced (every N days from last check-in), and Weekly Goalpost (e.g., 4×/week: partial XP per day + big bonus on quota!).

4. Auto & Manual Decay

Neglecting an ability past its grace period auto-drains XP, and you can log manual setbacks (bad exam, missed week). You can drop Ranks!
